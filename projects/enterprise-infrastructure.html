Enterprise Infrastructure Management

Managing and securing enterprise hybrid infrastructure end to end, spanning network operations, firewalls, VPNs, virtualization, Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Exchange Online, Intune, and cloud identity services.

Overview

As a Senior Network & Systems Administrator, I manage and secure hybrid enterprise infrastructure end to end, from the physical network layer through cloud identity and endpoint management. The environments span firewalls, switching, wireless, VPNs, VLANs, and DNS/DHCP; VMware and Hyper-V virtualization; and a Microsoft-centric cloud platform including Active Directory, Entra ID, Intune, Exchange Online, and Microsoft 365.

This isn't a single project. It is full operational responsibility for live enterprise environments, requiring simultaneous management across networking, virtualization, identity, endpoint, and cloud workloads while driving ongoing modernization initiatives.
